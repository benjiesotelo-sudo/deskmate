# MKT150 Chapter 1: Understanding Customers - Reading Notes

Term: 1st Semester 2026-2027. Materials version 1.

## Overview

Marketing begins with a specific person who has a specific problem, not with the product on the shelf.

## Main ideas

### Needs, wants and demand

A need is the underlying problem, a want is a preferred way to solve it, and demand is a want backed by the ability to pay.

### Segmentation

Divide a market into groups that behave alike, then decide which groups the business can serve best.

### The customer journey

People move from noticing a problem, to comparing options, to buying, to deciding whether to return.

### Listening

Short interviews with ten real customers usually teach more than a long survey sent to a thousand strangers.

## Key terms

- **Target market**: The group of customers a business chooses to serve.
- **Segment**: A group of customers with similar needs or behaviour.
- **Value proposition**: The short reason a customer should choose this offer over another.

## Worked example

A laundry shop near dormitories learns from interviews that students care most about same-day pickup. It stops advertising low prices and promises clothes back by 6 pm.

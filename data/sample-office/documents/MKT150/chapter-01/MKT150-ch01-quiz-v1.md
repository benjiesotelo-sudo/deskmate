# MKT150 Quiz 1: Understanding Customers

Term: 1st Semester 2026-2027. Quiz version 1. Time limit: 20 minutes. One attempt.

## Items

1. What is the difference between a need and a want?
2. What is a segment?
3. Name the stages of the customer journey.
4. Why interview a few real customers?

## Answer key (office copy, do not release)

1. A need is the underlying problem and a want is a preferred way to solve it.
2. A group of customers with similar needs or behaviour.
3. Noticing a problem, comparing options, buying, and deciding whether to return.
4. They reveal what actually matters, which large surveys often miss.

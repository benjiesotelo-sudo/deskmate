Office: Deskmate Demo Office

Syllabus version: 1

## Course description

An introduction to finding out what customers need, designing an offer around it, and reaching them through the right channels.

## Learning outcomes

1. Describe a target customer using needs, not demographics alone.
2. Design a basic marketing mix for a small product.
3. Choose digital channels that fit a customer and a budget.

## Schedule

- Weeks 1 to 3: Chapter 1, Understanding Customers. Quiz 1 is given on the Friday of week 3.
- Weeks 4 to 6: Chapter 2, The Marketing Mix. Quiz 2 is given on the Friday of week 6.
- Weeks 7 to 9: Chapter 3, Digital Channels. Quiz 3 is given on the Friday of week 9.

## Grading

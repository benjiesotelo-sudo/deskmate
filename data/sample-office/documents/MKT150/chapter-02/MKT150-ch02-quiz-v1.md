# MKT150 Quiz 2: The Marketing Mix

Term: 1st Semester 2026-2027. Quiz version 1. Time limit: 20 minutes. One attempt.

## Items

1. List the four elements of the marketing mix.
2. What does price communicate besides cost?
3. What is positioning?
4. Why must the four elements be consistent?

## Answer key (office copy, do not release)

1. Product, price, place and promotion.
2. A signal of quality.
3. The place an offer holds in the customer's mind relative to rivals.
4. Mixed signals confuse the target customer.

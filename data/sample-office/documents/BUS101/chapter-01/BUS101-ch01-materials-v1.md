# BUS101 Chapter 1: What a Business Does - Reading Notes

Term: 1st Semester 2026-2027. Materials version 1.

## Overview

A business exists to create value: it buys or makes something, adds work to it, and offers it to customers who think it is worth more than its price.

## Main ideas

### Value creation

A business combines inputs such as materials, labour and time into something a customer prefers over keeping their money.

### Revenue, cost and profit

Revenue is what customers pay. Cost is what the business gives up to serve them. Profit is what remains, and it is the signal that value was created.

### The four functions

Every business, however small, performs operations, marketing, finance and people management, even when one owner does all four.

### Goods versus services

Goods can be stored and counted. Services are produced and consumed at the same moment, so capacity and timing matter more than inventory.

## Key terms

- **Value**: The benefit a customer receives compared with the price paid.
- **Profit**: Revenue minus all costs for a period.
- **Stakeholder**: Anyone affected by what the business does, including customers, staff, owners, suppliers and neighbours.

## Worked example

A campus print shop pays 1.20 pesos for paper and ink per page and charges 3.00 pesos. The 1.80 peso difference pays the rent, the attendant and the owner. If students would rather walk to a cheaper shop, the value was not there.

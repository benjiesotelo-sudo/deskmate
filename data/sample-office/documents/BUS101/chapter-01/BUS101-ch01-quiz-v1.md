# BUS101 Quiz 1: What a Business Does

Term: 1st Semester 2026-2027. Quiz version 1. Time limit: 20 minutes. One attempt.

## Items

1. What is profit?
2. Name the four functions every business performs.
3. Why does timing matter more for a service than for a good?
4. A print shop spends 1.20 per page and charges 3.00. What is the margin per page?

## Answer key (office copy, do not release)

1. Revenue minus all costs for the period.
2. Operations, marketing, finance and people management.
3. A service is produced and consumed at the same moment and cannot be stored.
4. 1.80 pesos per page.

# BUS101 Foundations of Business - Course Syllabus

Office: Deskmate Demo Office
Term: 1st Semester 2026-2027
Course coordinator: Rafael Ignacio
Syllabus version: 1

## Course description

An introductory course on how a business creates value, how it is owned and organised, how it reports its results, and what it owes the people around it.

## Learning outcomes

1. Explain how a business turns inputs into value for customers.
2. Compare sole proprietorships, partnerships, corporations and cooperatives.
3. Read a simple income statement and compute gross and net profit.
4. Apply a stakeholder view to an everyday business decision.

## Schedule

- Weeks 1 to 3: Chapter 1, What a Business Does. Quiz 1 is given on the Friday of week 3.
- Weeks 4 to 6: Chapter 2, Forms of Ownership. Quiz 2 is given on the Friday of week 6.
- Weeks 7 to 9: Chapter 3, Reading a Simple Income Statement. Quiz 3 is given on the Friday of week 9.
- Weeks 10 to 12: Chapter 4, Ethics and Social Responsibility. Quiz 4 is given on the Friday of week 12.

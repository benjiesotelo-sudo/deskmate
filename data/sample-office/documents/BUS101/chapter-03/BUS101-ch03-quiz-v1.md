# BUS101 Quiz 3: Reading a Simple Income Statement

Term: 1st Semester 2026-2027. Quiz version 1. Time limit: 20 minutes. One attempt.

## Items

1. Sales are 200,000 and cost of goods sold is 120,000. What is gross profit?
2. What is the gross margin in that case?
3. Name two operating expenses.
4. Why must two income statements cover the same length of time before they are compared?

## Answer key (office copy, do not release)

1. 80,000.
2. 40 percent.
3. Any two of rent, salaries, utilities, marketing.
4. Because the statement reports a period, and different spans are not comparable.

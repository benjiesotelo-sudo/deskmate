# BUS101 Chapter 4: Ethics and Social Responsibility - Lecture Deck

Term: 1st Semester 2026-2027. Deck version 1. Prepared by Rafael Ignacio.

## Slide 1 - Why this chapter matters

A business keeps its licence to operate by treating the people it touches fairly, not only by obeying the law.

## Slide 2 - Law is the floor

Legal compliance is the minimum. Ethics covers the choices the law leaves open.

## Slide 3 - Stakeholder view

List who is affected by a decision before making it: customers, employees, suppliers, the community and the owners.

## Slide 4 - Conflicts of interest

When a personal gain could bend a business judgement, disclose it and step back from the decision.

## Slide 5 - Responsibility as strategy

Fair pay, honest labels and clean operations cost money now and build trust that is hard for rivals to copy.

## Slide 6 - Key terms

- **Ethics**: Principles for choosing what is right when rules alone do not decide.
- **Conflict of interest**: A situation where personal benefit competes with duty.
- **Corporate social responsibility**: The duty of a business to account for its effect on society and the environment.

## Slide 7 - Worked example

A canteen manager is offered a personal discount by a supplier bidding for the rice contract. The ethical path is to report the offer, decline it, and let someone else score the bids.

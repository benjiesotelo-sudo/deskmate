# BUS101 Chapter 2: Forms of Ownership - Lecture Deck

Term: 1st Semester 2026-2027. Deck version 1. Prepared by Rafael Ignacio.

## Slide 1 - Why this chapter matters

How a business is owned decides who controls it, who is liable for its debts, and how easily it can raise money.

## Slide 2 - Sole proprietorship

One owner, simple to start, full control, and unlimited personal liability for the debts of the business.

## Slide 3 - Partnership

Two or more owners share work, money and risk under an agreement. Each general partner can bind the others.

## Slide 4 - Corporation

A separate legal person owned through shares. Owners risk only what they invested, but the rules and reporting are heavier.

## Slide 5 - Cooperative

Owned by its members, who are also its customers or workers. One member, one vote, whatever the size of the share.

## Slide 6 - Key terms

- **Limited liability**: Owners can lose only the amount they invested, not their personal property.
- **Share**: A unit of ownership in a corporation.
- **Articles of partnership**: The written agreement that sets out contributions, duties and profit sharing between partners.

## Slide 7 - Worked example

Two friends open a milk tea stall as partners. When a supplier goes unpaid, the supplier may collect from either friend personally. Had they registered a corporation, only the money already in the stall would be at risk.

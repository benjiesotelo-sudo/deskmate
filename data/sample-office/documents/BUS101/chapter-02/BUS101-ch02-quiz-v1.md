# BUS101 Quiz 2: Forms of Ownership

Term: 1st Semester 2026-2027. Quiz version 1. Time limit: 20 minutes. One attempt.

## Items

1. Which form of ownership has unlimited personal liability with a single owner?
2. What does limited liability protect?
3. How are votes counted in a cooperative?
4. Give one disadvantage of a corporation.

## Answer key (office copy, do not release)

1. The sole proprietorship.
2. The personal property of the owners beyond what they invested.
3. One member, one vote.
4. Heavier rules, registration and reporting requirements.

# FIN201 Chapter 3: Credit and Borrowing - Reading Notes

Term: 1st Semester 2026-2027. Materials version 1.

## Overview

Borrowing moves future income into the present at a price, and that price is only visible when offers are compared on the same basis.

## Main ideas

### Good and bad reasons to borrow

Borrowing for something that lasts longer than the loan can make sense. Borrowing for something already consumed rarely does.

### Effective annual rate

Convert every offer to its yearly rate including fees. A low monthly rate can hide a high yearly cost.

### Minimum payments

Paying only the minimum on a card keeps the account in good standing and keeps most of the balance earning interest.

### Credit standing

Paying on time builds a record that lowers the cost of future loans.

## Key terms

- **Interest rate**: The price of borrowing, stated as a percentage per period.
- **Effective annual rate**: The true yearly cost of a loan after compounding and fees.
- **Collateral**: An asset pledged to the lender in case the loan is not repaid.

## Worked example

A lender advertises 2 percent a month. Compounded, that is about 26.8 percent a year, far above the 24 percent a quick multiplication suggests.

# FIN201 Quiz 3: Credit and Borrowing

Term: 1st Semester 2026-2027. Quiz version 1. Time limit: 20 minutes. One attempt.

## Items

1. A loan charges 2 percent a month. What is the effective annual rate, roughly?
2. What is collateral?
3. Why is paying only the minimum on a credit card costly?
4. Give one sensible reason to borrow.

## Answer key (office copy, do not release)

1. About 26.8 percent.
2. An asset pledged to the lender in case of non-payment.
3. Most of the balance keeps earning interest.
4. To buy something that lasts longer than the loan, such as education or equipment.

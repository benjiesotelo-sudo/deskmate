# FIN201 Chapter 1: Budgeting and Cash Flow - Lecture Deck

Term: 1st Semester 2026-2027. Deck version 1. Prepared by Lourdes Abad.

## Slide 1 - Why this chapter matters

A budget is a plan for where each peso will go before the month begins, checked against where it actually went.

## Slide 2 - Income first

List take-home pay and any allowance on the dates they arrive, not the amounts you hope for.

## Slide 3 - Fixed and variable spending

Fixed costs such as rent repeat every month. Variable costs such as food and transport can be steered.

## Slide 4 - The 50-30-20 guide

As a starting point, give about half to needs, about a third to wants, and at least a fifth to savings and debt payments.

## Slide 5 - Review weekly

Compare plan and actual every week. A budget that is only checked at month end is a history lesson, not a plan.

## Slide 6 - Key terms

- **Cash flow**: Money coming in minus money going out over a period.
- **Fixed expense**: A cost that stays the same from month to month.
- **Emergency fund**: Savings set aside for surprises, usually three to six months of needs.

## Slide 7 - Worked example

A new employee takes home 24,000 pesos a month. Using the 50-30-20 guide, needs get 12,000, wants get 7,200 and savings get 4,800. Rent of 7,000 and transport of 2,500 already use 9,500 of the needs budget.

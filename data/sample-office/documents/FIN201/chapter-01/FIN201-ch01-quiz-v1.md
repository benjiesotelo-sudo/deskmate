# FIN201 Quiz 1: Budgeting and Cash Flow

Term: 1st Semester 2026-2027. Quiz version 1. Time limit: 20 minutes. One attempt.

## Items

1. On a take-home pay of 24,000, how much goes to savings under the 50-30-20 guide?
2. Give one example of a fixed expense.
3. What is an emergency fund for?
4. Why review a budget weekly?

## Answer key (office copy, do not release)

1. 4,800 pesos.
2. Rent, a loan instalment or a subscription.
3. Unplanned expenses, usually sized at three to six months of needs.
4. So that spending can still be corrected before the month ends.

## Slide 1 - Why this chapter matters

A peso today is worth more than a peso next year because today's peso can earn interest in between.

## Slide 2 - Compounding

Interest earned is added to the balance and then earns interest itself. Growth speeds up the longer money is left alone.

## Slide 3 - Future value

Future value equals present value times one plus the rate, raised to the number of periods.

## Slide 4 - Present value

To find what a future amount is worth today, divide it by one plus the rate, raised to the number of periods.

## Slide 5 - The rule of 72

Divide 72 by the yearly interest rate to estimate how many years it takes money to double.

## Slide 6 - Key terms

- **Principal**: The original amount saved or borrowed.
- **Compound interest**: Interest computed on both the principal and the interest already earned.
- **Discounting**: Converting a future amount into its value today.

## Slide 7 - Worked example

10,000 pesos saved at 6 percent a year grows to 10,600 after one year and 11,236 after two years. By the rule of 72, it doubles in about 12 years.

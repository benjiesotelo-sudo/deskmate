# FIN201 Quiz 2: Saving and the Time Value of Money

Term: 1st Semester 2026-2027. Quiz version 1. Time limit: 20 minutes. One attempt.

## Items

1. What is 10,000 at 6 percent a year worth after two years?
2. Using the rule of 72, how long does money take to double at 8 percent?
3. What is discounting?
4. Why does compounding speed up over time?

## Answer key (office copy, do not release)

1. 11,236 pesos.
2. About 9 years.
3. Converting a future amount into its value today.
4. Because interest is earned on past interest as well as on the principal.

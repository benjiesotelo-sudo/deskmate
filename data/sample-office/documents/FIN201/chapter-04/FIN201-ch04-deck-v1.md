# FIN201 Chapter 4: Starting to Invest - Lecture Deck

Term: 1st Semester 2026-2027. Deck version 1. Prepared by Lourdes Abad.

## Slide 1 - Why this chapter matters

Investing means accepting uncertainty in exchange for the chance of growth, so the first decisions are about goals and time, not products.

## Slide 2 - Goal and horizon

Money needed within a year belongs in savings. Money not needed for five years or more can take on market risk.

## Slide 3 - Risk and return

Higher expected returns come with wider swings. Nobody is paid extra for taking no risk.

## Slide 4 - Diversification

Spreading money across many holdings reduces the damage any single one can do.

## Slide 5 - Costs compound too

A fee of 2 percent a year removes a large share of long-run growth. Compare fees before comparing past returns.

## Slide 6 - Key terms

- **Diversification**: Holding many different investments to reduce risk.
- **Time horizon**: How long the money can stay invested before it is needed.
- **Mutual fund**: A pooled investment managed on behalf of many investors.

## Slide 7 - Worked example

Two funds both earn 8 percent before fees. One charges 0.5 percent and the other 2 percent. Over 20 years, 100,000 pesos grows to about 425,000 in the first and about 321,000 in the second.
